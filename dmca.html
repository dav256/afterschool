Copyright & DMCA

Afterschool is a library of open-source games. Every game on this site was adapted from a project its creators released for reuse, and each one is credited in the site's README and in its own game folder.

If you own the rights to something on this site and believe it should not be here, send a takedown notice using the form below. Complete notices are reviewed promptly, and material that infringes is removed.

What a notice needs

  • Your full name and an email address where we can reach you
  • The copyrighted work you own (title and a short description)
  • The exact page address on this site where the material appears
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law
  • Your signature — typing your full legal name is fine — confirming the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or are authorized to act for the owner
Submit a takedown request

If your content was removed by mistake

Send a counter-notice through the same form with your name, the page address, and a statement that the material was removed in error. We will review it and restore the content where the law allows.

This page follows the notice-and-takedown process in the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). Submitting a false notice can carry legal penalties, so please only report material you genuinely own or represent.
